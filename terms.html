1. About SpringUp

SpringUp is a training management app for trampoline (TRA) and double mini-trampoline (DMT), for coaches and gymnasts. It is operated by the app developer, based in Portugal, reachable at the email address in section 17.

2. Who may use SpringUp

  • You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.
  • If you are between 13 and 17, you may only use SpringUp with the knowledge of a parent or legal guardian.
  • Coaches who invite minors to their club should make sure the gymnast's parent or guardian is aware of it.

3. Your account

  • Accounts are personal. Don't share your account or let someone else use it.
  • Keep your password safe and the information you provide accurate.
  • Tell us straight away if you think someone else is using your account.

4. Subscriptions

Some features need a paid plan. Plans are sold as subscriptions through the Apple App Store or Google Play and charged to your Apple or Google account.

  • Price: the price shown in the app, in your local currency, is the one you pay. Monthly and annual options are shown in the app.
  • Automatic renewal: subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel.
  • Cancelling: cancel in your App Store or Google Play settings at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.
  • Refunds: refunds are handled by Apple or Google under their own rules. SpringUp cannot issue refunds directly.
  • Price changes: we may change prices. You will be told in advance and can cancel before the new price applies.

5. Club plans and limits

  • The plan of a club belongs to the coach who created the club (the owner). Everyone in the club gets the features of the owner's plan. Gymnasts in a club don't pay.
  • Each plan has a member limit (gymnasts and extra coaches, not counting the owner), shown on the pricing page.
  • If the owner moves to a plan below the club's member count, nobody is removed, but new members can't join until the club is back under the limit.
  • If the owner's plan ends, the features that need a paid plan stop being available to the club until a plan is active again. Routines and physical stats stay free.

6. Free period at launch

People already using SpringUp when subscriptions launch get 14 days free: club owners get Coach 30+ and solo gymnasts get Solo Gymnast. After the free period, a paid plan is needed to keep the paid features.
