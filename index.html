Trampoline · DMT

Training management for trampoline and double mini-trampoline.

SpringUp is the app for TRA and DMT coaches and gymnasts. Log every session, measure time of flight, follow the training plan and run your club, all in one place.

Everything a training day needs

Built around how trampoline clubs actually train.

Training log

Sessions, turns per gymnast, attendance and notes, for the whole club or for your own training.

Time of flight

Read time of flight live from Veriflite sensors on your local network, with history per skill.

Training plans

Macro cycles with phases and weekly targets, assigned to gymnasts, with progress against the plan.

Club management

Create your club, invite gymnasts and coaches with a 6-character code and organise them in groups.

For coaches and for gymnasts

Coaches

  • Log club sessions, attendance and turns
  • A hub for each gymnast: history, time of flight, routines, plans, physical stats and notes
  • Routine builder for TRA and DMT, with execution scores
  • Competitions, evaluations and training load

Gymnasts

  • Join your club with the coach's code, or use SpringUp on your own
  • Your own training log and time of flight history
  • Routines, physical stats and evaluations
  • Competition history and the plans your coach assigns
