How plans work

The club's plan covers everyone

The plan of a club belongs to the coach who created it. Everyone in the club, gymnasts and coaches, gets the features of the owner's plan. Gymnasts in a club don't pay.

Always free

Routines and physical stats are free for everyone, with or without a plan.

Renewal and cancelling

Plans renew automatically. Cancel any time in the App Store or Google Play; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Changing to a smaller plan

If a club moves to a plan below its member count, nobody is removed. New members can't join until the club is back under the limit.

Already using SpringUp? When subscriptions launch, you get 14 days free: club owners get Coach 30+, solo gymnasts get Solo Gymnast.
